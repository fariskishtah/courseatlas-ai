## Dataset

60 hand-written questions (20 per course): 46 answerable (direct factual, concept, cross-document, CSV row, exact terminology, 1 ambiguous-but-answerable) and 14 that should be refused (6 unsupported by any course, 6 belonging to another course, 2 too vague; see `data/eval/questions.jsonl`). Gold labels are `(file, substring)` pairs written by hand and validated to exist in the files; they were **not** derived from system output. Questions alternate into `dev` (used only to calibrate the abstention threshold) and `test`.

Recall@k = at least one relevant chunk in the top k (for cross-document questions: *all* required gold groups covered). Relevance is binary, nDCG ideal = every relevant chunk in the index ranked first.

**Findings.** Chunk size 900/120 was best on every metric; 500 chars fragments evidence, 1500 dilutes it. Hybrid (BM25 + RRF) did **not** beat dense retrieval, so the simpler Standard-RAG retriever is the default. Retrieval latency is single-digit milliseconds because the index is tiny (128 chunks). With 46 questions, differences of 1-2 questions (≈0.02) are within noise.

**Known limitation (cross-document).** One-vector retrieval can miss the second source: for *how do the lecture notes and the exercise guide explain the mutable default bug*, the exercise-guide chunk ranks 7th (dense) at k=5-6; the integration test uses k=8.
